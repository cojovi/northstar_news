## Publisher disclosure

Cody Viveiros is the owner and publisher of The Northstar Ledger and [PROTOCOL SENTINEL](https://protocolsentinel.com). The publications share ownership. Coverage by one does not provide independent corroboration for the other. The publisher’s website is [cojovi.com](https://cojovi.com/).

This page is affiliated publisher information, replacing a promotional profile at the same URL. It is not independent profile reporting. It establishes the disclosed publication role, rather than educational qualifications, commercial results, awards or third-party acclaim.
