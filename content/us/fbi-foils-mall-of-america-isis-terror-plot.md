## What the initial complaint alleges

Sheikhdoon Abdullahi Mohamud was charged by complaint with one count of knowingly receiving a firearm and ammunition to commit a federal crime of terrorism, according to the [Justice Department’s October 7, 2026 release](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/man-arrested-and-charged-role-plotting-conduct-mass-shooting-event-support-isis-mall-america). DOJ reported his arrest on October 6 and initial appearance on October 7.

The [complaint and supporting affidavit](https://www.justice.gov/usao-mn/media/1464826/dl?inline=) allege communications about an ISIS-inspired attack at the Mall of America. DOJ says an undercover employee supplied a rifle rendered inert before the transaction, along with ammunition, and agents arrested Mohamud after the purchase.

The initial firearm-receipt count is brought under 18 U.S.C. § 924(h). The complaint references material support as an intended underlying terrorism offense; that does not make the initial count a standalone material-support charge.

These are prosecution allegations, not findings of guilt. Mohamud is presumed innocent unless proven guilty beyond a reasonable doubt in court. This account concerns the initial complaint; it does not report a subsequent court outcome or current docket status.
